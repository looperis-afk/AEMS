Reclaim Your Retail Labor.

AEMS is the intelligent middleware that cross-references POS velocity with Automated Replenishment data to locate hidden inventory before you lose the sale.

The 20th Century Method

Blind visual walks: Store associates waste upwards of 3 hours per day visually inspecting thousands of items, trying to guess what is missing from the shelf.

The Data Gap: Even when items are restocked, the central AR system never receives updated, separated counts. Corporate continues to blindly order freight you don't need.

The AEMS Solution

Algorithmic precision: AEMS constantly compares real-time register sales (POS) against backroom inventory to mathematically identify "Lies" in your data.

The Closed Loop: We push a targeted 45-minute picklist to existing associate handhelds. Upon scanning, our "Two-Bin" workflow permanently calibrates your corporate ordering algorithms.

Enterprise ROI, Achieved Instantly.

Zero CapEx Hardware

AEMS is lightweight middleware. It integrates directly with your existing POS streams and operates beautifully on the Zebra handhelds your team already owns.

3 Hours Saved Daily

By eliminating blind store walks and providing a prioritized, high-value picklist, management labor is instantly recovered for customer-facing activities.

Data Integrity Engine

Built-in UI safeguards prevent fat-finger entry errors. Hard-stops prevent overstocking beyond physical shelf capacity, protecting your backroom from freight overload.
